When you sign in, ArDrive opens **Your drives**, the list of all your drives. Open a drive to see its folders and files.

![Your drives, with the sidebar and a list of drives](/help/3-0/your-drives.png)

## The sidebar

The sidebar has these places:

- **Your drives.** Every drive in one list.
- **Public drives**, **Private drives**, **Shared with me**, and **Hidden.** The same drives, filtered. The number beside each counts the drives in it.
- **Web pages.** Everything you published. See [Web pages](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-web-pages).
- **Names.** The names you can point at your content. See [Names](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-names).

Click a drive in the sidebar to open it.

## Open and select

A single click selects a row. A double-click opens it: a drive or a folder opens, and a file opens its details panel with a preview. You can also click the menu at the right of a row, and click **Open** or **More info**.

## Your drives page

**Your drives** lists each drive with when it last synced on this device, how many files and how much it holds, and when you created it. A drive you haven't synced on this device shows "Not synced on this device". See [Sync Your Drive](/help/faqs-help-and-troubleshooting-guides-sync-your-drive).

## Inside a drive

The file list has **Name**, **Size**, **Last updated**, and **Date created** columns. The columns icon at the top right chooses which columns show, adds the **License** column, and turns on **Show hidden items**. The search box at the top searches files, folders, and drives.

For the actions on drives, folders, and files, see [Actions](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-actions). To add things, see [The New button](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-the-new-button).
