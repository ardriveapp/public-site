Attaching a drive adds a public or private drive that someone shared with you to your account. Attaching a drive costs nothing, and so does downloading its files. You need the drive ID. A private drive also needs its access key.

## Attach a drive

1. Click **New**, then click **Attach drive**.
2. In **Drive ID**, paste the drive ID.

   ArDrive looks the drive up. When it finds the drive, it fills in the drive's name and shows whether it is public or private and who owns it.

   ![Attach drive, with the drive found and its name filled in](/help/3-0/attach-drive-found.png)

3. Optional: change the name in **Drive name**. The name is only yours. It doesn't change the owner's name for the drive.
4. Click **Attach**.

The drive opens, and ArDrive adds it to **Shared with me** in the sidebar.

## Attach a private drive

When the drive is private, ArDrive says "This is a private drive. Enter its access key to continue." Enter the key the owner sent you, then click **Attach**. Without the key, you can't open the drive.

## Find an attached drive

Click **Shared with me** in the sidebar. The number beside it counts the drives you attached.

## Open a drive without attaching it

To look at a shared drive without an account, see [Open a drive without signing in](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-open-a-drive-without-signing-in).
