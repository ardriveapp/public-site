Share a drive or a folder with a link. The person who opens the link doesn't need an ArDrive account.

## Share a public drive

1. Open the drive.
2. Click the menu at the right of the drive name, and click **Share drive**.
3. In **Share drive with others**, click **Copy link**, then click **Done**.

Anyone with the link goes straight to the drive, with no account or login. See [Open a drive without signing in](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-open-a-drive-without-signing-in).

## Share a folder

1. Click the menu at the right of the folder's row, and click **Share folder**.
2. In **Share folder with others**, click **Copy link**, then click **Done**.

The dialog says "Anyone with the link can open this folder."

![Share folder with others, with the link hidden](/help/3-0/share-folder.png)

## Share a private drive

A private drive is encrypted. The link on its own doesn't open it. The person you send it to also needs the drive access key.

1. Open the drive.
2. Click the menu at the right of the drive name, and click **Share drive**.
3. Click **Copy link** to copy the **Link**.
4. Click **Copy key** to copy the **Drive access key**. The key is hidden. Click the eye icon to show it.
5. Send the link and the key. Send the key apart from the link.

![Share drive with others for a private drive, with the link hidden and the Drive access key masked](/help/3-0/share-private-drive.png)

The link alone doesn't open the drive. ArDrive says so under the options: "The link alone won't open this drive. The person you send it to also needs the access key above."

### Include the key in the link

The option **Include the key in the link** is off when the dialog opens. If you turn it on, anyone with the link can open every file in the drive, including files you add later. ArDrive warns you of this. A drive key can't be changed, so you can't take access back from someone who has it.

![Include the key in the link, with the warning that anyone with the link can open every file](/help/3-0/share-private-drive-key-in-link.png)

### Share a folder in a private drive

**Share folder** in a private drive works the same way. The access key opens everything in the drive, not just that folder. Share the key only with people you trust with the whole drive.

## Good to know

- The person you share a private drive with can't open it again if they lose the key. Send it again.
- Don't share private drives or files on public platforms.
- Sharing doesn't cost anything.
- To attach a shared drive to your account, see [Attach a drive](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-attach-a-drive).
