You can hide files, folders, and drives. A hidden item leaves your normal view. Hiding changes what ArDrive shows. It doesn't remove data from Arweave.

## Hide an item

1. Click the menu at the right of the file, folder, or drive.
2. Click **Hide**.

ArDrive hides the item at once. For a drive, the menu is at the right of its row in **Your drives**, or at the right of the drive name when the drive is open.

Hiding is a small write. When your free upload allowance covers it, ArDrive makes the change with no review. When it doesn't, ArDrive shows a review with the price first and uses Turbo Credits.

## Show hidden items

1. Click the columns icon at the top right of the file list.
2. Click **Show hidden items**.

![The Columns menu with Name, Size, Last updated, Date created, License, and Show hidden items](/help/3-0/columns-menu.png)

Hidden files and folders show dimmed. Hidden drives show in the sidebar with a hidden icon. Click **Show hidden items** again to hide them from view.

## Unhide an item

1. Show hidden items.
2. Click the menu at the right of the hidden item, and click **Unhide**.

## Hidden drives

Click **Hidden** in the sidebar to see every hidden drive. The menu of a hidden drive has **Unhide**.

![The Hidden list with one hidden drive and its menu showing Unhide](/help/3-0/hidden-drives.png)

## What hiding doesn't do

- Hidden data in a public drive can still be found, because the data is public. Hiding only changes your view.
- ArDrive leaves hidden items out of [published pages](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-publish-to-web) and [deployments](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-create-an-arweave-manifest).
- A name still opens a hidden file. When you hide a file that has a name, ArDrive asks **Remove its name too?** so you can remove it. See [Names](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-names).
