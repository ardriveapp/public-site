**Account and security** shows how you sign in, what ArDrive remembers on this device, and the addresses behind your account.

## Open the page

1. Click your account at the top right.
2. Click **Account and security**.

![Account and security with Sign in, This device, and Addresses](/help/3-0/account-keyfile.png)

## Sign in

This section depends on how you sign in.

| You sign in with | The section shows |
|---|---|
| A keyfile or a recovery phrase | **Keyfile**, with a **Download** button |
| Wander | "Wander keeps your key. Back it up there." and **Connected** |
| Phantom or Solflare | "Phantom signs you in. Your files stay with this wallet." and **Connected** |

To save a copy of your keyfile, click **Download**. Keep the keyfile private and somewhere safe. Anyone who has it can control your wallet.

## This device

**This device** shows how many private drives ArDrive remembers on this device, such as "3 drives remembered". A remembered drive opens without its password.

To forget them:

1. Click **Forget**.
2. In the confirmation, click **Forget**. ArDrive says "You'll need their passwords to open them again."

For how remembering works, see [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).

## Addresses

- **Arweave, for your files.** The address of your account. Click the copy icon to copy it.
- **Solana, for names.** The Solana wallet that holds your names. Click **Link** to add one. See [Names](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-names).

If you sign in with Phantom or Solflare, the Solana address is the wallet you signed in with. ArDrive labels it **Solana, for sign-in and names**.
