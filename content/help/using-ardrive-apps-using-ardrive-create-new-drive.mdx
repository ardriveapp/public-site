A drive is a container for your files and folders. You need a drive before you upload files.

## Create a drive

1. Click **New**, then click **New drive**.

   ![The Create drive dialog with the Private privacy option selected](/help/3-0/create-drive.png)

2. In **Drive name**, enter a name.

3. In **Privacy**, choose **Private** or **Public**. **Private** is selected by default.

   - **Private.** Files in a private drive are encrypted. Only people you share them with can open them.
   - **Public.** Anyone can find a public drive and view its files.

4. Click **Create drive**.

The drive appears in **Your drives**, and in the sidebar under **Public drives** or **Private drives**. Open it to add folders and upload files. See [Uploading files](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-uploading-files).

## Before you choose

- You can't change a drive between public and private. Choose **Private** for anything that you don't want others to read.
- Data on Arweave is permanent, so a public drive's files can't be deleted.
- Your drive password protects your private drives. If you sign in with a wallet, ArDrive asks you to set it when you make your first private drive. See [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).
