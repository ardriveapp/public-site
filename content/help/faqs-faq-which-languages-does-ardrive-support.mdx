Which languages does ArDrive support?

-   English, Spanish, Hindi, Japanese, and Chinese (Simplified and Hong Kong).

Where do I choose the language?

-   ArDrive has no language setting. It uses the language of your browser. If your browser's language is one of the supported languages, ArDrive opens in it. Otherwise it opens in English.

How do I change the language ArDrive shows?

-   Change the language list in your browser's settings, put the language you want first, and reload ArDrive. Your browser's own help explains where the language list is.

![The ArDrive landing page in Spanish](/help/3-0/language-spanish.png)

Is everything translated?

-   Most of the text is. The translations are made by machine. Dates and some messages stay in English, and a few words, such as "or" on the landing page, can stay in English.

Does the language change my files?

-   No. The language only changes the text of the app. File names and drive names stay as you wrote them.

If a translation is wrong or unclear, tell us on the [feature request](/help/faqs-faq-where-can-i-make-a-feature-request) page.
