## Snapshots are rollups of your Drive - it makes syncing your Drive in future sessions much faster.

**QuickSync** a roll up of your Drive's ArFS metadata into a single snapshot, meaning much faster syncing times for your Drive in future sessions.

Snapshots help users with large drives. You can create them for public and private drives. They shorten the sync time of a drive you share or attach.

1.  Open the drive, click **New**, click **Advanced**, and click **New snapshot**. You can also click the menu at the right of the drive name, and click **Create snapshot**.
2.  Follow the steps. ArDrive determines the size and cost of the snapshot and shows them before it uploads.
3.  Upload the snapshot. The next time you sync, the drive syncs faster.

If recent changes to the drive are still being confirmed on Arweave, ArDrive says so, for example "4 recent changes to this drive are still being confirmed on Arweave. Try again in a few minutes." Wait, then try again.

Syncing from snapshots is on by default. To change it, open **Settings** and see **Sync from snapshots**. See [Sync Your Drive](/help/faqs-help-and-troubleshooting-guides-sync-your-drive).
