Turbo Credits pay for uploads in ArDrive. You pay once for each upload, with no subscription. You can add credits from the upload review, from your profile panel, or from the Billing page.

## The free upload allowance

Your free upload allowance covers small uploads and small changes, such as hiding and unhiding a file. When an upload is within it, the upload review shows **Free** as the cost and says "Covered by your free upload allowance." When a publish or similar action is within it, the review shows **Cost: 0 credits** and says that uploads this small are free with Turbo.

When you have used it up, ArDrive says so, and the action needs credits.

## Add credits when you upload

The upload review shows the cost of your files. If the upload is over your free upload allowance and you don't have enough credits, ArDrive asks you to add them without leaving the upload.

1. Click **New**, then click **Upload files**. Choose a drive if ArDrive asks where.
2. Click **Choose files** and select your files.

   ![The upload review showing the cost for one file](/help/3-0/upload-review.png)

3. Read the cost. If the upload needs more credits than you have, ArDrive says "This upload is over your free upload allowance" and shows how many credits it needs.

   ![The upload review with a warning that the upload is over the free upload allowance, and an Add credits button](/help/3-0/upload-add-credits.png)

4. Click **Add credits**.
5. In **Add Turbo Credits**, choose an amount: **$10**, **$25**, **$50**, or **$100**. ArDrive shows how many credits you get and whether that is enough for the upload.
6. Click **Continue to payment** and pay by card.
7. Back in the upload review, click **Upload**.

## Add credits from your profile or Billing

1. Click your account at the top right.
2. Click the **+** next to **Credits**, or click **Billing** and then **Add credits**.

   ![The profile panel with Credits, Account and security, Billing, and Settings](/help/3-0/profile-menu.png)

3. In **Buy Turbo Credits**, choose a **Payment method**: **Credit/debit card** or **Crypto**.
4. Choose how to pick the amount in **Select by**: **Storage** or **USD**.
5. Choose an amount, or enter a custom amount from $5 to $10,000. If you have a promo code, click **Have a promo code?**.

   ![The Buy Turbo Credits dialog with a $10 amount and the purchase summary](/help/3-0/add-credits-dialog.png)

6. Check the **Purchase summary**. It shows the credits you get, roughly how much storage they buy, and the price.
7. Click **Continue** and complete the payment.

### Pay with crypto

Under **Crypto**, choose one of these:

- USDC on Base
- ETH on Base
- USDC on Ethereum
- ETH on Ethereum
- SOL

ArDrive refreshes the price before you pay and never pays an expired quote. If you decline in your wallet, ArDrive says no payment was made. If a top-up is already open in another tab, ArDrive warns you.

## See your balance and purchases

Open **Billing** from your profile panel.

![The Billing page balance, with the numbers hidden](/help/3-0/billing-balance.png)

- **Balance.** Your **Turbo Credits** and about how much storage they buy. If credits are shared with you, ArDrive splits the balance into yours and shared with you. It also shows your AR, which pays for uploads directly.
- **Purchases.** Each purchase with its type (such as **Card** or **Crypto**), amount, date, receipt, and credits. Click **Download CSV** to save your purchases.
- **Shared credits.** Credits shared with you and credits you shared, with how much is left or used, and when each share expires.

If you signed in with Phantom or Solflare and credits are held on that wallet, Billing shows them as "credits on your wallet" with a **Use them** button. Click it and approve in your wallet to use those credits in ArDrive. For credits on a MetaMask wallet, Billing says to share them in the ar.io Console. Purchases you make with those wallets in the ar.io Console show in the ar.io Console.

## Share credits

You can share your credits with another person. The person you share with can spend from your credits until you revoke the share or it expires. Billing lists the credits you shared and shows how much of each share is used.

## Credit expiry and refunds

Credits expire twelve months after the date of purchase. All sales are final. For the full terms, see the [Terms of Service](/tos-and-privacy). For answers to common questions, see the [Turbo FAQ](/help/faqs-faq-ardrive-turbo).

## Pay with AR instead

If you hold AR, ArDrive can use it to pay for uploads directly. Billing shows your AR balance. See [Fund your wallet](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-fund-your-wallet).
