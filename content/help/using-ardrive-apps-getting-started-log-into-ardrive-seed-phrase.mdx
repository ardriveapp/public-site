Your recovery phrase is the 12 words you saved when you made your account. You can log in with it on any device, without a keyfile.

## Log in with a recovery phrase

1. Go to [app.ardrive.io](https://app.ardrive.io) and click **Log in**.

2. Click **Recovery phrase or keyfile**.

3. In **Enter your recovery phrase**, type your 12 words in order, separated by spaces. To check what you typed, click **Show**.

   ![The Enter your recovery phrase panel](/help/3-0/signin-keyfile.png)

4. Click **Continue**.

5. Enter your password and click **Continue**.

   If this wallet has used ArDrive before, ArDrive asks for the password you used then. If ArDrive doesn't recognize the wallet, it asks you to choose a password.

ArDrive opens **Your drives**.

If ArDrive says "That isn't a recovery phrase. Check each word.", compare your words with the ones you saved. See [Login trouble](/help/faqs-help-and-troubleshooting-guides-login-trouble).

To learn how recovery phrases work, see [How do keyfile and recovery phrase login work](/help/faqs-faq-how-do-keyfile-and-seed-phrase-login-work).
