Rename a file, a folder, or a drive from its menu.

## Rename a file or folder

1. Click the menu at the right of the file or folder, and click **Rename**.
2. In **Rename file**, change the name. For a folder the dialog says **Rename folder**.
3. Click **Rename**.

![The Rename file dialog with the current name](/help/3-0/rename-file.png)

**Rename** stays off until the name is different.

You can also open the details panel with **More info**. The pencil icon in its toolbar renames the item.

## Rename a drive

Click the menu at the right of the drive name, and click **Rename drive**.

## Edit a note

A text note has **Edit note** in its menu instead of **Rename**.

## What happens to the data

Data stored in ArDrive can't be changed. A rename saves a new record that uses the new name with the file's existing data. The earlier name stays in the history. This lets you keep your files organized after you upload them.

Renaming is a small write. When your free upload allowance covers it, it costs nothing. Otherwise it uses Turbo Credits.

To learn how ArFS records changes, see the [ArFS documentation](https://ardrive.io/arfs).
