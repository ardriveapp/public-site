There are two ways to pay for permanent storage with ArDrive.

1. **Turbo Credits.** Pay by card or with crypto and ArDrive uses the credits for your uploads. You don't need to manage AR. Small uploads are covered by your free upload allowance.

   To add credits, see [Add Turbo Credits and use Billing](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-add-turbo-credits-and-billing). For common questions, see the [Turbo FAQ](/help/faqs-faq-ardrive-turbo).

2. **Arweave tokens (AR).** Use AR from your Arweave wallet.

   ArDrive is built on the [Arweave network](https://arweave.org/), and the AR token pays the global network of operators who secure the network and provide storage.

   To use AR, buy it through an exchange or swap service that is available in your region. Then transfer it to your Arweave wallet address. Check wallet addresses carefully before you send funds.
