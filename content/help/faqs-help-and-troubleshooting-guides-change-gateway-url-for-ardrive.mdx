ArDrive connects to Arweave gateways to read files, to look up your drives, files, and folders, and to send uploads. Changing a gateway can help if the default gateway is unavailable, or your internet provider blocks it.

This setting is for advanced users. Changing it can affect loading, syncing, previews, and how soon uploads appear in the app. The default gateway is `https://turbo-gateway.com`.

## Change the gateway in Settings

1. Click your account at the top right, then click **Settings**.
2. Under **Network**, find the setting you want to change:
   - **Gateway.** "Where ArDrive reads files from."
   - **GraphQL server.** "Where ArDrive looks up your drives and files."
   - **Turbo services.** "Where uploads and payments are sent."
3. Click **Change** below it.
4. In **Switch gateway**, enter the URL in **Gateway URL**, or click **Choose an ar.io gateway**.
5. Click **Save**.

![The Network section of Settings, with Gateway, GraphQL server, and Turbo services](/help/3-0/settings-network.png)

![Switch gateway, with the Gateway URL field and Choose an ar.io gateway](/help/3-0/switch-gateway.png)

Settings says "Advanced. Change these only if you know you need to."

After you change the gateway, run a full resync so ArDrive rebuilds its view. See [Sync Your Drive](/help/faqs-help-and-troubleshooting-guides-sync-your-drive).

## Change the gateway before you log in

You can change gateway settings from the login screen.

1. Click the settings icon at the top right of the welcome screen.
2. Click **Set gateway**, **Set GraphQL server**, or **Set Turbo services**.

![The settings menu on the welcome screen with Set gateway, Set GraphQL server, Set Turbo services, and Sync all drives on login](/help/3-0/login-settings-menu.png)

The same menu has the **Sync all drives on login** switch and the ArDrive version. The welcome dialog that asks for your password has the same settings icon.

## After an update

If you chose a custom gateway before ArDrive 3.0, check **Settings**. The update can reset saved settings, including the gateway, to the defaults.

## When to try this

Try another gateway if you troubleshoot syncing, previews, or gateway availability. If an outage lasts longer, visit our [contact page](/contact) before you change settings.
