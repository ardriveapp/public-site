I didn't get my recovery phrase from ArDrive. Can I still use it to log in?

-   If you have a recovery phrase from another Arweave-compatible wallet, such as Wander, you can use it to log in to ArDrive.

Can I get a recovery phrase for my existing wallet?

-   No. If you did not save your recovery phrase when the wallet was created, it cannot be generated or recovered later.

What is a recovery phrase?

-   A recovery phrase is a set of random words generated when you create a wallet. You can use it to log in or as a backup to regain access to your account.

What do I do if I lose my recovery phrase?

-   You can still log in if you have your keyfile. If you lose both your recovery phrase and keyfile, your wallet cannot be recovered. Keep your recovery phrase in a safe place.

How do I keep my recovery phrase safe?

-   Write it down on paper and store it securely, or save it in a password manager.

Is logging in with a recovery phrase safe?

-   Yes. Always verify you are on [ardrive.io](https://ardrive.io) or one of the [official ArDrive dapps](/help/faqs-faq-ardrive-on-the-permaweb-dapp) before entering your recovery phrase so you do not share it with another site or app.

Why is my wallet generated every time I log in with a recovery phrase?

-   Your recovery phrase identifies and regenerates your wallet each time you log in to ArDrive. Your wallet is required to access your drives, upload and sign transactions, and check your balance.

What format does my recovery phrase need to be in to log into ArDrive?

-   Enter the 12 words in the order they were given to you, separated by spaces.

Can I use my ArDrive-generated wallet in Wander?

-   Yes. A wallet generated by ArDrive works with other apps in the permaweb ecosystem, including Wander.
