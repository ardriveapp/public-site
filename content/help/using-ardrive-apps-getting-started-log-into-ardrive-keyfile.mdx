A keyfile is a file that holds your Arweave wallet. You can log in with one from an ArDrive account, or from a wallet made in another app such as Wander.

## Log in with a keyfile

1. Go to [app.ardrive.io](https://app.ardrive.io) and click **Log in**.

   ![The ArDrive log in panel with the Recovery phrase or keyfile option](/help/3-0/signin-options.png)

2. Click **Recovery phrase or keyfile**.

3. Click **Choose a keyfile** and select the keyfile on your computer.

   ![The recovery phrase panel with the Choose a keyfile button](/help/3-0/signin-keyfile.png)

4. Enter your password and click **Continue**.

   If this wallet has used ArDrive before, ArDrive asks for the password you used then. If ArDrive doesn't recognize the wallet, it asks you to choose a password.

ArDrive opens **Your drives**. Your password also opens your private drives. See [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).

If ArDrive says "That file isn't a keyfile. Choose the keyfile you saved.", the file you picked isn't a wallet keyfile. See [Login trouble](/help/faqs-help-and-troubleshooting-guides-login-trouble).

## Log in again on the same device

ArDrive remembers your account on this device. The next time you open it, **Welcome back** asks for your password.

![The Welcome back panel asking for a password](/help/3-0/signin-welcome-back.png)

To use another account, click **Use a different account**.

To learn how keyfiles work, see [How do keyfile and recovery phrase login work](/help/faqs-faq-how-do-keyfile-and-seed-phrase-login-work).
