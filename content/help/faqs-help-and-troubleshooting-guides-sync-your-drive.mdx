Syncing brings ArDrive up to date with your drives on Arweave. If your drive looks stale, recently uploaded files don't appear, or files show red or orange status longer than expected, run a sync.

Some problems come from gateway indexing delays and clear on their own. A sync gives ArDrive another chance to ask the gateway and update your view.

## Sync your drives

1. Open **Your drives**.
2. Click **Sync all drives**.

To sync one drive, open its menu and click **Sync this drive**.

The sync menu is the arrow beside **Sync all drives**. It has these items:

- **Sync all drives.** "Gets changes since the last sync."
- **Full resync all drives.** "Rereads all history. Slower; use it if something looks missing."
- **Sync history.** What the most recent syncs did.

![The sync menu with Sync all drives, Full resync all drives, and Sync history](/help/3-0/sync-menu.png)

While a drive syncs, ArDrive shows how far it is. Click **Stop syncing** to end it. ArDrive keeps what finished. If a sync fails, the sync menu offers **Try again**.

A drive you haven't synced on this device shows "This drive isn't synced on this device". Click **Sync this drive** to see its files and folders.

## When to use Sync

Use **Sync** when:

- Your drive mostly looks right, but recent files or folders are missing.
- A file status hasn't updated after waiting for gateway indexing.
- You reloaded ArDrive and want it to check for changes.

## When to use Full resync

A full resync rereads the whole history of your drives from Arweave, without using snapshots. Use **Full resync** when:

- **Sync** doesn't find the missing files or folders.
- Your drive looks incomplete or out of date.
- You changed gateways and want ArDrive to rebuild its view of your drive.
- Support asks you to run a full resync.

To resync one drive, open its menu and click **Full resync this drive**.

A full resync takes longer than a sync, especially for large drives. ArDrive asks you to confirm first. The message says "Nothing on this device is deleted."

![The Full resync confirmation](/help/3-0/full-resync.png)

## See what happened

Click **Sync history** in the sync menu. It lists the most recent syncs on this device, with the drive, when it ran, who started it, how long it took, and how many items changed. ArDrive keeps the last 20 and clears them when you log out.

![Sync history, listing recent syncs](/help/3-0/sync-history.png)

## Sync automatically

Open **Settings** from your account menu. Under **Sync**:

- **Automatic sync** checks your drives for changes every 5 minutes while ArDrive is open.
- **Sync all drives when I sign in** syncs every drive at sign-in. When it is off, ArDrive reads only the drive you open.
- **Sync from snapshots** loads large drives faster from a saved copy. Turn it off to read every change from the start.

## When sync doesn't fix it

If the gateway hasn't indexed the file, folder, or upload yet, a sync can't find it. Wait a few minutes to a few hours, then try again.

If you keep seeing gateway, GraphQL, 502, 503, 504, or bad gateway errors, see [Bad Gateway And Network Errors](/help/faqs-help-and-troubleshooting-guides-bad-gateway-and-network-errors).

Advanced users can change the gateway. See [Change Gateway URL For ArDrive](/help/faqs-help-and-troubleshooting-guides-change-gateway-url-for-ardrive).
