---
title: "ArDrive Explorer View"
description: "Where to find the actions for your drives, folders, and files in ArDrive."
category: "Using ArDrive Apps"
section: "Actions"
---

Each drive, folder, and file has one menu with its actions, such as **Share**, **Publish to web**, **Download**, **Rename**, and **Move**. Click the menu at the right of a row to open it.

For an overview, see [Drive Explorer](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-drive-explorer).
