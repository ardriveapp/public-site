After you [create a drive](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-create-new-drive), you can upload files to it. Folders keep the drive organized.

## Create a folder

1. Open the drive, or the folder where the new folder should go.
2. Click **New**, then click **New folder**.
3. Enter a name and click **Create folder**.

The new folder appears in the list. Double-click it to open it. The names above the list are breadcrumbs. Click one to go back up to that folder.

## Share, move, and rename a folder

Click the menu at the right of the folder's row. Its items include **Share folder**, **Publish to web**, **Download**, **Rename**, **Move**, **Hide**, and **More info**. To share a folder, see [Drive sharing](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-drive-sharing).
