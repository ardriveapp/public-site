You can open a drive that someone shared with you without an account. You only need its link or its drive ID.

## Open a drive

1. Go to [app.ardrive.io](https://app.ardrive.io).
2. Click **Open a drive without logging in**.
3. In **Drive link or ID**, paste the link or the drive ID.

   ArDrive looks the drive up and shows "Drive found" with its name and whether it is public or private.

   ![Open a drive, with the drive name found under the field](/help/3-0/open-drive-found.png)

4. Click **Open drive**.

The drive opens under **Shared with me**. You see its files and folders. A **Log in** button at the top right lets you sign in when you want to.

![A public drive opened without signing in, with Shared with me in the sidebar and a Log in button](/help/3-0/open-drive-visitor.png)

## Open a private drive

A private drive also needs the drive key it was shared with. When you paste a private drive, ArDrive says "This is a private drive. Paste the drive key you were given." Paste the key in **Drive key**, then click **Open drive**.

If the key is wrong, ArDrive says "This key doesn't open this drive." Ask the person who shared the drive to send the key again. They should send it apart from the link.

The shared link of a private drive opens the drive on its own only if the sender included the key in the link. See [Drive sharing](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-drive-sharing).

## If ArDrive can't open it

| ArDrive says | What to do |
|---|---|
| No drive was found for that ID | Check the link, or ask the person who shared it. |
| That doesn't look like an ArDrive link or drive ID | Paste the whole link, or the 36-character drive ID. |
| That's a link to a file | Click **Open file** to see the file. |
| That's a link to a transaction | Click **View transaction**. |
| Couldn't reach the network | Check your connection and try again. |

## Keep a drive

If you have an account, you can add a shared drive to it. See [Attach a drive](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-attach-a-drive).
