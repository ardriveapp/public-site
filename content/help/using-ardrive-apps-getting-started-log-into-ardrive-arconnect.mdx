Wander is a Chrome browser extension that holds your Arweave wallet. You sign in to ArDrive by approving in Wander. There is no ArDrive password to remember.

Wander keeps your key. Back it up in Wander.

## Install Wander

1. Go to the [Wander website](https://arconnect.io) and download the Wander Chrome extension.
2. Add the extension to Chrome.
3. In the Wander welcome page, create a password for Wander. This password is not an ArDrive password. Wander can't recover it.
4. Create a new wallet or load one you already have.

   To make a new wallet, copy the recovery phrase and download the keyfile, and keep both somewhere safe. They can't be recreated.

## Log in with Wander

1. Go to [app.ardrive.io](https://app.ardrive.io) and click **Log in**.
2. Under **or a browser wallet**, click **Wander**.
3. In Wander, approve the request to connect to ArDrive.
4. In Wander, approve the request to sign in to ArDrive.

ArDrive opens **Your drives**. While ArDrive waits, it shows **Approve in Wander**.

## Open private drives

Wander accounts use a drive password for private drives. You set it the first time you need a private drive. See [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).

A drive that isn't open yet shows **Locked**. With Wander, one approval can open many locked drives at once. Click **Unlock** and approve in Wander.

## If Wander doesn't answer

- **Wander didn't answer.** Open Wander and check that it is unlocked. Then try again. If nothing opens, restart your browser.
- **Wander needs your permission again.** Click **Reconnect** and approve in Wander.
- **Wander isn't available.** Install or turn on Wander in this browser, then try again.
- **Signed out.** Your Wander account changed. Log in again.

For more help, see [Login trouble](/help/faqs-help-and-troubleshooting-guides-login-trouble).
