Credit sharing lets one wallet spend from the Turbo Credits of another, up to an amount the owner approves. The credits stay with their owner. A share is not a transfer: the person you share with can spend credits, and can't take them.

ArDrive shows shares on the **Billing** page.

## See your shares

1. Click your account at the top right.
2. Click **Billing**.

If credits are shared with you, **Balance** splits your **Turbo Credits** into "yours" and "shared with you". Uploads can spend both.

The **Shared credits** section lists each share:

- **Shared with you.** Credits another wallet shared with you.
- **You shared.** Credits you shared with another wallet.

Each share shows how much is left, such as "0.5 left", or how much is used, such as "0.2 of 1 used". It also shows when the share ends, such as "expires" and a date. A share with no end date shows "never expires". A share that has ended shows "expired".

## Share credits with another wallet

Use the ar.io Console to share credits with another wallet. Billing lists the share after you make it.

## Use credits held on your Solana wallet

If you sign in with Phantom or Solflare and you bought Turbo Credits with that wallet in the ar.io Console, those credits belong to your Solana wallet, not to the Arweave address ArDrive uses for uploads. **Billing** shows them in a row such as "1.5 credits on your Phantom wallet".

1. Click **Use them**.
2. Approve in your wallet.

ArDrive then shares those credits with your account, and they count as credits shared with you.

If you sign in with MetaMask, Billing says to share the credits in the ar.io Console, and shows a button to copy your Arweave address. Share them to that address.

## When credits expire

Credits expire twelve months after the date of purchase. A share has its own end date, which Billing shows. A share that says "never expires" has no end date, but the credits in it still expire twelve months after they were bought. For the full terms, see the [Terms of Service](/tos-and-privacy).
