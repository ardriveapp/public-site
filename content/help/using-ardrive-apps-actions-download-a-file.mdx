---
title: "Download A File"
description: "Download files from ArDrive. Downloading costs nothing."
category: "Using ArDrive Apps"
section: "Actions"
---

1. Click the menu at the right of the file, and click **Download**.
2. The file downloads to your computer.

You can also open the details panel with **More info**. The download icon in its toolbar does the same.

For a private file, ArDrive removes the encryption when it downloads.

Folders and drives have **Download** in their menus too. A download of many files is saved as a zip. A drive also has **Export contents**, which saves a table of its files. See [Export a CSV table](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-export-csv-table).

There is no charge to download, preview, or share your files in ArDrive.
