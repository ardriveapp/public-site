One password protects all your private drives. ArDrive can't change or recover it. If you lose it, you can't open your private drives, so keep it somewhere safe.

## Which password you use

The password depends on how you sign in.

| How you sign in | Your drive password | When ArDrive asks |
|---|---|---|
| Keyfile, recovery phrase, or an account made in ArDrive | Your sign-in password | Nothing more is asked |
| Wander, Phantom, Solflare, or MetaMask | A password you set | The first time you need a private drive |

A wallet account signs in with an approval in the wallet, so it has no sign-in password. To sign in with a wallet, click **Wander**, **Phantom or Solflare**, or **MetaMask** under **or a browser wallet** on the log in panel, then approve in the wallet. See [Log into ArDrive with Wander](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-log-into-ardrive-arconnect) and [Log into ArDrive with MetaMask](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-log-into-ardrive-metamask-wallet).

## Set a drive password

If you sign in with a wallet, ArDrive asks you to set the drive password when you first need a private drive.

1. Create a private drive. See [Create a drive](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-create-new-drive).
2. In **Keep your files private**, enter a password of at least 12 characters. To get one made for you, click **Suggest a strong password**. ArDrive says "Use at least 12 characters." until the password is long enough.
3. Enter the same password in **Confirm password**.
4. Click **Set password**. The button stays off until both passwords match.

![The Keep your files private dialog with Password and Confirm password fields](/help/3-0/keep-your-files-private.png)

Use a password you can keep. It protects every private drive you make later.

## Unlock a locked drive

A drive shows **Locked** when ArDrive can't open it yet. A locked drive appears as **Private drive**, with a lock, until you unlock it.

1. Open **Your drives**.
2. In the locked drive's row, click **Unlock**. You can also double-click the row. A single click only selects it.
3. In **Unlock drive**, enter your drive password.

   ![The Unlock drive dialog with Try on my other locked drives and Remember on this device selected](/help/3-0/unlock-drive.png)

4. Check the two boxes under the password. **Try on my other locked drives** and **Remember on this device** are both selected when the dialog opens. Clear either one that you don't want.
5. Click **Unlock**.

If you open a locked drive from **Private drives** in the left menu instead, ArDrive shows **This drive is locked** with an **Unlock** button. Click it to open the same dialog.

If ArDrive says **Wrong password**, check the password and try again.

**Try on my other locked drives** tries the same password on your other locked drives, so one password can open them all. It appears when you have other locked drives.

A drive made before ArDrive 3.0 can have its own password. For these drives, the dialog says "The password you used with ArDrive before". Enter that password.

With Wander, **Unlock** asks for your drive password first, and then Wander shows one **Sign items** request. Approve it. That one approval opens your other locked drives only if they use the same password.

If you sign in with a password and it doesn't open any private drive, ArDrive shows **This password didn't open your private drives**. Check the password and try again, or continue. Drives it doesn't open stay locked until you unlock them.

## Remember on this device

**Remember on this device** keeps a private drive unlocked on this device, so you don't unlock it again. The unlock dialog selects it for you. Clear it on a device you don't trust, because anyone who can use your browser can open a remembered drive.

To see whether a drive is remembered:

1. Open the drive's menu and click **More info**.
2. Find **On this device**. It shows **Remembered** or **Not remembered**.

![The drive details with On this device showing Remembered and the Forget on this device button](/help/3-0/drive-remembered.png)

To forget one drive, click **Forget on this device**. The drive stays open until you close the session, and it is locked the next time.

To forget every remembered drive:

1. Click your account at the top right and click **Account and security**.
2. Under **This device**, find the number of drives remembered and click **Forget**.
3. In the confirmation, click **Forget**. ArDrive says "You'll need their passwords to open them again."

![The Account and security page with the This device section](/help/3-0/account-security.png)

Clearing your browser's site data also forgets remembered drives. See [Clearing your cache](/help/faqs-help-and-troubleshooting-guides-clearing-your-cache).
