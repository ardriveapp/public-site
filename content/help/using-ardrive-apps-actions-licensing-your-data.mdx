A license says how other people can use your file. ArDrive offers the Universal Data License (UDL) and the Creative Commons licenses. A license is saved as a small record next to the file, and anyone who can open the file can read it.

## Add a license

1. Click the menu at the right of the file or folder, and click **Add license**.
2. In **License**, choose **Creative Commons (CC)** or **Universal Data License (UDL)**.
3. Click **Next**.
4. Set up the license:
   - For Creative Commons, choose a **Type** such as **Attribution** or **Attribution Non-Commercial**.
   - For the UDL, enter a **License fee** and a **Currency**, and set **Commercial use** and **Derivations**.
5. Click **Next**.
6. In **Review license**, check the license and the **Cost**. Click **View** to read the license text.
7. Click **Confirm**.

![Review license, with the license and a cost of Free](/help/3-0/license-review.png)

## What a license costs

A license is a small write, and the review states what it costs. When your free upload allowance covers it, the review says **Free** and "Covered by your free upload allowance." Turbo decides what the allowance covers. When it doesn't, the review shows a price and the license uses Turbo Credits. See [Add Turbo Credits and use Billing](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-add-turbo-credits-and-billing).

## See a license

Anyone who opens a file can read its license on the file's **Details** tab.

To see licenses in the file list, click the columns icon, then click **License**. The **License** column shows only when something in the list has a license.

## Creative Commons licenses

Creative Commons is a nonprofit that offers standard licenses. You choose which freedoms to give others. The licenses range from the most open, which allow commercial use and changes, to the most restrictive, which let others share your work if they credit you and don't change it or use it commercially. CC0 Public Domain Dedication gives up all rights in a work.

To choose, use the [Creative Commons License Chooser](https://chooser-beta.creativecommons.org/). To learn more, see [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/cclicenses/).

## Universal Data License (UDL)

The UDL is a license for content on public blockchain networks. Its terms address the way data is stored there. To learn more, see [the Universal Data License explained](https://dev.to/fllstck/the-universal-data-license-explained-2di).

## Impacts of licensing

- **Clarity.** A license tells others what they can and can't do with your content.
- **Control.** You choose how your content is used.
- **Sharing.** A license that allows reuse adds to the commons.
