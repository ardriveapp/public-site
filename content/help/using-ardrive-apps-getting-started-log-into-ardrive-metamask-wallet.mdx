You can sign in to ArDrive with MetaMask. ArDrive makes an Arweave wallet for your account from a signature in MetaMask, so you don't need another wallet extension or keyfile.

## Log in with MetaMask

1. Go to [app.ardrive.io](https://app.ardrive.io) and click **Log in**.
2. Under **or a browser wallet**, click **MetaMask**.
3. In MetaMask, approve the request to connect to ArDrive, and the signature request. The signature is free and sends no transaction.
4. When ArDrive asks **Have you used ArDrive with this MetaMask before?**, choose one:

   - **No, I'm new.** ArDrive makes a new, empty account for this MetaMask.
   - **Yes.** Choose this if you made your MetaMask account before ArDrive 3.0 and set a password. ArDrive asks for that password.

   ![The question Have you used ArDrive with this MetaMask before, with No, I'm new and Yes](/help/3-0/metamask-used-before.png)

5. If ArDrive shows **Approve in MetaMask**, approve the sign-in requests in MetaMask.

ArDrive opens **Your drives**. A new MetaMask account has no ArDrive password.

Approve only signature requests that ArDrive asks for.

## Private drives

To make a private drive, you set a drive password the first time you need one. See [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).

## Keyfile and account details

A MetaMask account has no keyfile to download. Open **Account and security** to see that MetaMask signs you in and that your files stay with this wallet.

1. Click your account at the top right.
2. Click **Account and security**.

## Pay for uploads

To pay for uploads, add Turbo Credits. See [Add Turbo Credits and use Billing](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-add-turbo-credits-and-billing).
