Each drive, folder, and file has one menu with its actions. The menu is the same wherever the item appears. Click the three dots at the right of a row to open it. For a drive that is open, the menu is at the right of the drive name.

## Files

![A file's menu](/help/3-0/menu-file.png)

A file's menu has these items:

- **Preview.** Opens a public file at its web address. See [Public preview](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-public-preview).
- **Share file.** See [File sharing](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-file-sharing).
- **Publish to web.** See [Publish to web](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-publish-to-web).
- **Download.** See [Download a file](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-download-a-file).
- **Rename.** A text note has **Edit note** instead. See [Rename a file or folder](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-rename-a-file-or-folder).
- **Move.** See [Move a file or folder](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-move-a-file-or-folder).
- **Add license.** See [Licensing your data](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-licensing-your-data).
- **Hide** or **Unhide.** See [Hiding files](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-hiding-files-from-your-drive).
- **More info.** Opens the details panel.

A private file has no **Preview**. Its **Publish to web** makes a file page that opens only with the key in its link.

## Folders

![A folder's menu](/help/3-0/menu-folder.png)

A folder's menu has **Open**, **Share folder**, **Publish to web**, **Download**, **Rename**, **Move**, **Add license**, **Hide**, and **More info**.

## Drives

![A drive's menu](/help/3-0/menu-drive.png)

A drive's menu has **Share drive**, **Publish to web**, **Download**, **Export contents**, **Rename drive**, **Create snapshot**, **Hide**, **Sync this drive**, **Full resync this drive**, and **More info**. A drive that is already published shows **Published to web**. A private drive has no **Publish to web**. See [Export a CSV table](/help/using-ardrive-apps-actions-export-csv-table) for **Export contents**.

## The details panel

Double-click a file, or click **More info**, to open its details panel. The panel has **Preview**, **Details**, and **Activity** tabs. A toolbar at the top has the main actions as icons: share, download, preview, rename, move, and hide. Point at an icon to see its name.

![The toolbar of the details panel, with Share file, Download, Preview, Rename, Move, and Hide](/help/3-0/file-panel-toolbar.png)
