**Keyfiles:**

A keyfile is a file that contains your Arweave wallet. The wallet identifies you when you sign in to ArDrive and signs your transactions.

When you sign in with a keyfile, ArDrive asks for your password. ArDrive remembers your account on this device. The next time you open ArDrive, **Welcome back** asks for your password. To remove the wallet from this device, click **Use a different account**.

Anyone with your keyfile can use your account. Keep it in a secure location and don't share it. ArDrive can't recover a lost keyfile. You can download your keyfile from **Account and security** if you signed in with a keyfile, a recovery phrase, or an account made in ArDrive. ArDrive asks for your password first.

**Recovery phrases:**

A recovery phrase is 12 words that act as a backup of your wallet. When you enter the phrase, ArDrive makes your wallet from it, the same one every time. You can download that wallet as a keyfile.

You enter your recovery phrase and your password to sign in. Once you are in, you can see your balances, use your drives, and upload.

Anyone with your recovery phrase can use your account, so keep it private. We recommend a password manager. ArDrive can't recover a lost phrase.

**Wallets:**

Wander, Phantom, Solflare, and MetaMask sign you in with an approval in the wallet. There is no ArDrive password to sign in. The wallet keeps your key, so back it up there. For private drives, you set a drive password. See [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).

**Remember on this device:**

ArDrive can remember a private drive on a device you trust, so you don't unlock it each time. You can make this device forget it from the drive details or from **Account and security**. See [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).
