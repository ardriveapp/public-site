You sign up with a password and a recovery phrase. ArDrive makes your Arweave wallet on your device, so your account key never leaves it.

Have a password manager or a safe place for paper ready. ArDrive can't recover your password or your recovery phrase.

## Sign up

1. Go to [app.ardrive.io](https://app.ardrive.io) and click **Sign up**.

   ![The ArDrive welcome panel with Log in and Sign up buttons](/help/3-0/signin-landing.png)

2. Wait while ArDrive creates your account. This can take up to a minute. Keep the tab open.

   If your browser has a wallet extension, ArDrive first shows **Create your account**. Click **Create an account**. To use the wallet instead, see [Log into ArDrive with Wander](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-log-into-ardrive-arconnect), [MetaMask](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-log-into-ardrive-metamask-wallet), or Phantom or Solflare in [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).

3. In **Choose a password**, enter a password of at least 12 characters. To get one made for you, click **Suggest a strong password**.

   ![The Choose a password dialog with a strong password entered](/help/3-0/signup-password.png)

4. If you used a suggested password, click **Copy** and save it in your password manager.

5. Confirm the password and click **Continue**.

6. In **Save your recovery phrase**, click **Show**, and write down the 12 words in order. Or click **Copy** and save them in your password manager.

   Optional: click **Download a keyfile instead** to save a keyfile for your wallet.

   ![The Save your recovery phrase panel with the words hidden](/help/3-0/signup-recovery.png)

7. Select **I've saved my recovery phrase. ArDrive can't recover it.** and click **Continue**.

You are signed in. ArDrive then sets up a private drive named My files for you. If it can't, your first upload creates it.

## What each secret does

- **Password.** It protects your private drives, and you use it to sign in. See [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).
- **Recovery phrase.** The 12 words let you sign in on any device. They don't open your private files without your password.
- **Keyfile.** A file that holds your wallet. You can download it at any time from **Account and security**. See [Log into ArDrive with a keyfile](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-log-into-ardrive-keyfile).

Nobody can recover a lost recovery phrase.
