Phantom and Solflare are browser wallets that hold Solana keys. You sign in to ArDrive by approving in the wallet. ArDrive makes an Arweave account for your files from your Solana wallet, so there is no ArDrive password to sign in.

## Before you start

Install Phantom or Solflare in your browser and unlock it. ArDrive shows the sign-in option only when it finds one of them.

## Log in

1. Go to [app.ardrive.io](https://app.ardrive.io) and click **Log in**.
2. Under **or a browser wallet**, click **Phantom or Solflare**.

   ![The Log in panel with Recovery phrase or keyfile, and Phantom or Solflare under or a browser wallet](/help/3-0/signin-phantom-option.png)

3. Approve the requests that your wallet shows. ArDrive asks the wallet to connect and to sign a message. While it waits, it says **Approve in Phantom** or **Approve in Solflare**.
4. Wait while ArDrive says **Setting up your account.** The first sign-in takes longer.

ArDrive opens **Your drives**. A new account shows **Getting started**, where you can upload files or create a drive.

If ArDrive says **Login failed**, close the message, check that your wallet is unlocked, and try again.

The next time you open ArDrive, **Welcome back** shows **Continue with Phantom** or **Continue with Solflare**. Click it and approve in your wallet. To use another account, click **Use a different account**.

## Your two addresses

**Account and security** shows two addresses for a Phantom or Solflare account:

- **Solana, for sign-in and names.** The address of your wallet. It also holds your names. See [Names](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-names).
- **Arweave, for your files.** The address ArDrive uses for your files and uploads.

![Account and security for a Phantom account, with the Solana and Arweave addresses hidden](/help/3-0/account-phantom.png)

## Private drives

A wallet account has no sign-in password, so ArDrive asks you to set a drive password the first time you need a private drive. See [Your drive password](/help/using-ardrive-apps-getting-started-ardrive-password).

## Turbo Credits held on your wallet

If you bought Turbo Credits with your Solana wallet in the ar.io Console, **Billing** shows them as credits on your wallet, with a **Use them** button. See [Share Turbo Credits](/help/using-ardrive-apps-using-ardrive-share-turbo-credits).
