## What to report

Report phishing, malware, scams, child sexual abuse material and other abuse that you find in:

* Files and drives shared through ArDrive.
* Content served from ardrive.net or turbo-gateway.com, or from any of their subdomains.

## How to report

Email [abuse@ardrive.io](mailto:abuse@ardrive.io). Include:

* The full URL where you found the content.
* The Arweave transaction ID, if you have it.
* What the content does, such as phishing, malware or a scam.
* For phishing, the brand or service it imitates.

## What happens to reported content

Data stored on Arweave is permanent and cannot be deleted. Reported content can be blocked on the gateways we operate, so that it no longer loads at the reported address.

## Child sexual abuse material

Report it to [abuse@ardrive.io](mailto:abuse@ardrive.io). You can also report it to the [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org/).
